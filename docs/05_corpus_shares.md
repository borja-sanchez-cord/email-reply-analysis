# Corpus shares (G=30) — how representative are the studied emails?

Study window Jan 2025 – Jul 2026: **85,696 fresh pushes**.

## Opener channel by year (share of pushes)

| year | mailbox | sequencer | other |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | **75%** (31,441) | 20% (8,253) | 5% |
| 2026 (Jan–Jul) | **28%** (12,491) | 72% (31,423) | 0.1% |

The studied corpus (mailbox openers) is **most of 2025 outbound but under a third of
2026 outbound** — outbound roughly doubled as Amplemarket ramped, and the growth went to
the sequencer. Goes near the top of the write-up.

## Eligibility waterfall (study openers, G=30)

- 85,696 pushes → 43,932 mailbox openers → **38,154 clean** (bounced −2,717; incoming
  from the person within the 30-day gap −~2,590; opener was a reply into an existing
  thread −~2,280; prospect-started −79; some overlaps).
- Of clean mailbox openers, **8,983 from confirmed CAs** (team-membership evidence);
  ~29k from senders needing identity/role resolution (marketing identified and excluded;
  archived owners resolved separately — see docs/06).
- Sync check: 40 sender-months with zero inbound visibility, removing 3,363 mailbox sends
  (2.6%) from Question 1.

## Q2 visibility (good news)

Within clean mailbox-opened pushes, 96.4% have *only* mailbox touches (mean sequencer
share of follow-up touches: 2.5%) — the sequencer-reply-invisibility problem barely
affects the follow-up curve for this corpus.
