--- email (subject: Mission 3A | Encord - meeting follow-up) ---
Hi Estefan and Tracey,

Great reconnecting last week. I know you’ve made good progress on the AWS
side so I wanted to follow up with a few resources to help you get data
flowing into Encord.

*Step 1: Ingesting from your AWS buckets*
Now that the integration is live, you're ready to start uploading video and
other assets. There are a few different ingestion modes depending on your
preferences:

   - *Cloud Synced Folder*: If you’d like your AWS bucket to auto-sync with
   Encord (including new files as they arrive), this is the most seamless path.
   - *Strict Client-Only Access*: If you’d prefer to manage access tightly
   and avoid direct integration, we support pre-signed URLs for controlled
   ingestion.
   - *JSON-based Uploads*: For more control, you can register files via
   JSON in bulk, including custom metadata like resolution, FPS, etc.

I recommend having a look at the attached tech onboarding doc (slides 8-11
in particular) as it has more detail on each of these options.

*A few suggestions based on our last call:*

   - Since you're planning to upload multi-view surgical footage, I’d
   strongly recommend enabling Data Groups
   <https://docs.encord.com/sdk-documentation/index-sdk/sdk-data-groups#create-data-groups>.
   These let you group files together into a single task and ensure they’re
   synced during annotation.
   - It sounds like you're also thinking about audio ingestion and
   diarization.  We support MP3 ingestion today, and I’d love to hear a bit
   more about how you’re planning to use speaker identification so we can
   point you in the right direction.

Thanks again! Around if any questions come up.

Dave
