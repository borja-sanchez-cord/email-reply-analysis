C. WHY DOES TEMPLATING COST 2-4x? six probes
==============================================================================

C1. is a why-now missing from templates?  [DEAD END]
  templated 70.7% vs hand-written 71.8% — identical

C2. does the why-now work less well inside templates?  [DEAD END — noise]
  hand-written why-now +5.1pp; extra when templated -2.0pp p=0.216 -> not distinguishable
  2x2 (still the useful slide):
                         size  reply%
T            why_now                 
hand-written no why-now   674     5.8
             why-now     1712    10.7
templated    no why-now   812     3.6
             why-now     1955     5.4

C3. is it just worse text?  [PARTIAL — 27% explained, 73% is not the text]
  sender FE only: -5.40pp p=0.0000
  + all 19 measured text features: -3.95pp p=0.0036
  share of penalty explained by measurable text: 27%

C4. does it scale with copies sent?  [DEAD END — flat, so it's a cliff not a slope]
                  size  reply%
template_repeats              
1                 2028    10.3
2                  358     3.9
3-4                393     4.6
5-9                631     5.5
10-24              734     3.3
25+               1009     5.6
  per doubling of copies: +0.24pp p=0.3309 -> no dose-response

C5. spam filtering?  [WEAK EVIDENCE AGAINST — no company-size gradient]
  <200 emp   n=2101 template -4.75pp p=0.000
  200-1999   n=1746 template -5.09pp p=0.000
  2000+      n=1228 template -6.15pp p=0.015
  (bigger firms have stronger filters; penalty barely moves -> humans, not filters)

C6. reply LATENCY — the one positive clue
  templated median 4.8d (n=134) vs hand-written 1.8d (n=222)
  -> mail lands and is read, then deprioritised. Filtering would not slow replies.

C7. are some templates good and most dead?  [DEAD END — mostly chance]
  173 templates; zero-reply observed 66% vs 60% expected by chance
  overdispersion chi2=263 df=172 p=1.03e-05 -> templates differ, but only 1.29x chance
