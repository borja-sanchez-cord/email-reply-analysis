  bespokeness = 'could this have been sent to someone else unchanged?'
  Among emails WITH a specific reason (grade 4-5):
    hand-written n=1479  mean besp 3.63  top-2-box  58.9%  reply  11.8%
    templated    n=1165  mean besp 3.32  top-2-box  48.5%  reply   6.3%

  If the seam were the mechanism, a specific reason inside a BESPOKE body
  should outperform one inside a generic body. It does not:
    hand-written bespoke  11.8% (n= 871)   generic  11.8% (n= 608)
    templated    bespoke   3.9% (n= 565)   generic   8.5% (n= 600)

    bespoke body: inbound-triggered share   0.0%

    bespoke body:   3.9% (n= 565)
